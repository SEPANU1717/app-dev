# Alice in Borderland
**Alice in Borderland** is a Japanese suspense thriller series based on the manga by Haro Aso. It combines survival games, mystery, and psychological drama.

## Plot Summary
Arisu, a young gamer, finds himself transported to a mysterious version of Tokyo called the "Borderland." Here, he must participate in deadly games to survive.

## Main Characters
- **Ryohei Arisu** – The main protagonist, smart but struggling in real life.
- **Usagi Yuzuha** – A skilled climber who becomes Arisu’s ally.
- **Chishiya** – A mysterious and intelligent player with a calm demeanor.
- **Kuina** – Brave and athletic, she joins Arisu’s group.

## Key Features
- ⚔️ Deadly games with high stakes  
- 🧩 Strategic thinking and teamwork  
- 🎭 Intense psychological drama  
- 🌆 Futuristic and dystopian Tokyo setting
